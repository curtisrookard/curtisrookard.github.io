## Research interests

My research focuses on intelligent and resilient approaches to cybersecurity, particularly where machine learning interacts with networked, distributed, and resource-constrained systems.

### Current themes

- Reinforcement learning for intrusion detection and cyber defense
- Federated learning for distributed security analytics
- Robustness against adversarial attacks
- IoT and industrial-control-system security
- Sequential and recurrent models for cyber-threat detection
- Network resilience and complex systems

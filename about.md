## Interests 

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, data science, and cybersecurity. I am especially interested in methods that make cyber-defense systems more adaptive, robust, and useful in operational settings.

My technical interests include:

- Machine learning and deep learning for intrusion detection
- Reinforcement learning for cyber defense
- Federated and privacy-aware learning
- Adversarial machine learning
- Internet of Things and cyber-physical security
- Networked and distributed systems

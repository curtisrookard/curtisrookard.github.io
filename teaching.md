My teaching interests include cybersecurity, information technology, computer systems, programming, networking, and applied machine learning.

## Subject areas

- Cybersecurity fundamentals and network defense
- Computer operations and operating systems
- Information technology foundations
- Python programming
- Networking and systems administration
- Machine-learning fundamentals
- Security certification preparation

## Teaching philosophy

I aim to connect technical concepts to realistic problems, emphasize hands-on practice, and help students understand both how systems work and how they fail.
